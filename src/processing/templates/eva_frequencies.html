How to read these counts

This is a categorical frequency bar chart. EVA encodes written shapes, not known sounds. The choice of what constitutes a single manuscript character is unresolved.

Grouped forms uses longest-match segmentation for cfh, ckh, cph, cth, ch and sh; every remaining EVA letter is counted individually. For example, cth is one token, while iin remains three. Individual letters counts c, t and h separately. A zero row means absent under that counting rule; c and h are often parts of groups.

All LINES text loci are included (paragraph text, labels and other transcribed items). Headers, comments and inline angle-bracket annotations are removed. ALT ambiguous bracketed alternatives are omitted in full rather than choosing a reading. RARE extended @number; codes are excluded. Curly braces marking ligatures are removed while their contents are retained. Other punctuation and unreadable markers break sequences; no group can cross those boundaries. Case is normalized. The 25 EVA font letter codes a–v and x–z are listed, including zeros. This is a basic-EVA reference, not a catalogue of every rare or extended sign. Percentages are rounded.

Fallback: the default chart is already written into this HTML. If scripting is disabled or initialization fails, it remains visible. All counts and glyph outlines are embedded; no data fetches, font downloads, chart libraries, or network connection are needed.
