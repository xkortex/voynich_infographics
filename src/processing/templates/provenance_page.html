Cleaning and counting

EVA frequencies remove ambiguous bracketed spans and extended codes, retaining other readable spans. Word positions and word lengths omit entire words containing uncertain or extended material. Both grouped and letter-level EVA views are conventions, not decipherments.

Position charts use 15 equal bins of token index / (word length − 1); one-token words are placed in the middle. Word-length comparisons count repeated word occurrences. The supplied Forme edition includes editorial notes and glossary; Orationum includes front matter, apparatus and OCR noise. These are full-edition comparisons, not isolated recipes and prayers.

Known provenance limits

The exact Orationum OCR is fingerprinted, but the user confirms it was produced with Tesseract and Poppler using an assistant-provided script. The identified script, ocr_fullres_mac.py, is preserved unchanged in the repository. Its defaults are 400 DPI, grayscale PNG, lat+fra+eng, OEM 1 and PSM 3; the actual invocation, installed tool versions and the PDF checksum remain unrecorded. The user identified the source PDF as this CCSL 160 scan. Obtaining the same book and running fresh OCR will not necessarily reproduce these counts. Its redistribution permission has not been established.
