Methods, controls and limits

What is fitted? Four related EVA resources (ZL, IT, VT, RF basic) and the full supplied Forme of Cury and Corpus orationum editions. IT and VT share ancestry; RF combines readings. Full-edition editorial material and Latin OCR errors remain. Voynich retains complete basic-EVA words only. Accents and historical letters remain in English and Latin; apostrophes do not count as units. EVA grouping uses ch, sh, cfh, ckh, cph, cth. This is an exploratory implementation, not a numerical reproduction of a published Voynich experiment.

Sukhotin. Symmetrize the within-word pair-count matrix, zero its diagonal, select the largest positive contact sum, then subtract twice that symbol’s contacts from remaining sums. Repeat until no positive sum remains. Selected symbols form A. Ties are lexical. This follows Mayer (2010), §3.2; its first selected symbol is the contact-frequency seed.

HMM. A two-state character-emission model, with independent word starts and no boundary symbol. Scaled forward/backward expectation-maximization fits transition, start and emission probabilities. Train on up to 3,000 words evenly sampled across each dataset, excluding words longer than 30 units; preserve repeated occurrences. Three seeded random starts, at most 25 EM iterations; retain the greatest training likelihood. No vowel references enter fitting or restart selection. A is the state with at least half the expected occurrences of Sukhotin’s seed symbol. Assign other symbols by their expected A-occurrence proportion ≥ 0.5; these are fitted memberships, not calibrated vowel probabilities. Unseen symbols remain unassigned. Training likelihoods compare initializations on the same sample, not different corpora.

Resampling. Four seeded block-bootstrap runs resample complete folios with replacement for Voynich, or artificial contiguous 200-word blocks for Forme and Orationum. Sukhotin uses all resampled words; each HMM uses up to 1,000 evenly sampled words and one initialization. HMM labels align to the full fit’s anchor, or weighted full-fit agreement when the anchor is absent. Sukhotin retains its algorithm-selected class. Bar denominators count only runs where the symbol was observed. This mixes sampling and fitting sensitivity; four runs are a diagnostic, not a confidence interval or a manuscript-section classification. Rare-symbol results are particularly fragile.

Controls. Shuffle preserves each word’s unit inventory and length but randomizes order per occurrence. Vowel deletion removes a/e/i/o/u, accented forms and æ/œ from the comparison corpora; y and Latin consonantal/vocalic i/u are not contextually annotated. This is an orthographic deletion control, not an authentic abjad. Interior scope removes the first and last current units after shuffling/deletion and drops empty results. It does not pair across original word boundaries.

Reading the result. Alternation is the fraction of all within-word pairs with two assigned symbols in different classes. A frequency-only reference, 2p(A)p(B), is shown as a rough diagnostic; within-word shuffle is the direct control. A clean split can reflect affixes, boundary positions or glyph components. Agreement with familiar vowel letters is a control check, not validation of a Voynich decipherment.
