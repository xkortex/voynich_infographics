What you’re reading

A reading edition, not a decipherment.

This page uses the supplied Voynichese Project XML, rather than the Zandbergen–Landini transcription used in the charts. Text and order follow the XML. Original line breaks, paragraph structure, drawings, and the placement of labels are not reconstructed. Spelling differences between editions are expected.

Each word opens Voynichese on its folio with an exact-spelling query. Repeated copies of the same spelling are all highlighted; the viewer’s link format does not identify a unique XML word occurrence. Viewer availability is outside this site’s control.

Glyph outlines are derived from Gabriel Landini’s EVA font. Connected forms use the font’s capitals (Sh, cFh, cKh, cPh, cTh). The rendering is a typographic aid, not a diplomatic reproduction. The existing glyph copyright and rights caveat applies.
